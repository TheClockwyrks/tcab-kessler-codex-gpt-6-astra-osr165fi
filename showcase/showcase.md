# Kessler

The sky is full of yesterday's machines. You're here to take them apart. Circle a living planet with a single deflector and send a demolition ball back into three rings of abandoned satellites.

Every return is a decision. Catch the ball at the center for a clean radial strike, or use the edge to bend its course into another patch of wreckage. The outer rings counter-rotate; their gaps open, close, and carry your next target past you.

Wreckage carries opportunities. Catch salvage to widen your deflector, split the attack into multiple balls, raise a last-chance shield, or punch straight through armor. Watch the coral pods: a narrow deflector makes the next return much less forgiving.

Clear the entire sky and another, faster wave takes its place. You have three lives and no finish line. Keep the ball away from the planet. Make every orbit count.
